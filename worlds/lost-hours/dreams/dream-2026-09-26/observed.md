## Verdict

Yes, mostly. The village remembers through its authored surfaces, and that works:

- the ledger
- two new cards in the grown room, with my name on one of them
- Umber's pinned label
- Mott's chip with my name on it
- Tace's Marlow and humming chips
- Bell's Vesper chip and ribbon
- Linden's OONA saucer
- the nap asleep with my hush

Every player from yesterday is named somewhere, for the specific thing they did. The note named me too. On the rubric, "being remembered" goes from 3/5 yesterday to 4/5 today.

What keeps it from a 5:

- Free-text conversation does not know what the chips know. NPCs mostly echo what I tell them, and Mott's "remember me" answer is yesterday's line again.
- Bell reintroduced herself as if we had never met.
- Tace, Tock and Bell still use the same entry lines word for word.

Speech handling and reply speed are clearly fixed.
