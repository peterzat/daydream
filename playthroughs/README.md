# Playthroughs (spoilers)

> **Spoiler warning.** These are blind playthroughs of The Village of Lost Hours, and most of them walk through its puzzles and their solutions step by step. If you would like to find your own way through the village, you may want to play it first and come back afterwards.

Each `<date>-<name>.md` is one run's report, written by a model that had never seen the code, the story or these notes, after it played the game in a browser. A session record follows the report; the harness writes it, not the player (moves, minutes, how many screenshots the player opened, page and server errors). The folder of the same name holds the run's think-aloud notes, its action log and every screenshot it saw (`shots/`, numbered by move; after the first three runs, shrunk to 960 px wide for sharing).

The first run, `2026-10-01-priya`, could also ask its browser for the page's text, and opened few of its screenshots (its session record says how few); every run after it played by sight alone, the screenshot its only view.

The players and their accounts are made up, each played its own throwaway village on the box, and the action logs mask the made-up password. Read a report as one first-time player's evening, not as a review: its claims are spot-checked against the screenshots before they become fixes, and its suggestions are the player's own.
