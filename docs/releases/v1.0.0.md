daydream 1.0: a cozy storybook world that grows from the inside — running
entirely on one 20 GB GPU, with no cloud calls.

A stranger on the tailnet can enter the dream, pick a toon with a painted
watercolor face, play the clockmaker quest to its authored chapter close,
plant a dreamseed and find the world grew a room that may hold another
seed, leave and return to a journal that remembers their story — and, in
the bundled Zork I world, win visibly.

## The four flagships

- **The world grows as a loop.** A planted dreamseed composes a small
  persistent room inside authored boundaries (one local-LLM call, strict
  validation, every failure path preserves the seed) — and can now leave a
  fresh dreamseed resting inside the room it grew (authored chance,
  generation ceiling, deterministic seeded roll). Plant, wander in, find
  the next seed.
- **The cast has faces.** Toons are lazily painted as watercolor
  portraits (SDXL + the watercolor LoRA, a dedicated portrait workflow
  with face-aware negatives) through the same cached, provenance-recorded
  pipeline as room art. Faces appear live in the scene margin and the
  character picker.
- **The book remembers your story.** Leaving the dream writes a short
  past-tense journal recap of your own events (validated, capped,
  idempotent, fail-closed). Returning shows a "previously, in your
  dream…" beat, and the satchel's collection page is your real story.
- **Endings are visible; newcomers are welcomed.** Winning reaches every
  connected player as a dismissible "The End" storybook page (score,
  rank; the world keeps running), and a first-visit "How to Dream" leaf
  explains the five basics, reachable anytime behind the footer's `?`.

## Also in 1.0

- Zork I ships as pure world data on Zork-agnostic engine primitives; the
  350-point walkthrough replays deterministically with zero LLM calls,
  live over WebSocket, and GREEN against the real 1980 game via the
  dfrotz differential oracle.
- The shipped Clockmaker's Loft world carries authored per-NPC ambient
  drift pools, the propagation config, and a one-time first-planting
  chapter close (WORLD_VERSION 1.4).
- Hardening: regen-UI kill switch, delete grace window, loopback-only
  world swap, a benign-refusal probe that attributed 0/21 fallbacks on
  live NPC dialogue, and a `tests/security/` suite.
- CI (lint + the GPU-free tier on Python 3.10/3.12), ~1250 tests, MIT
  license, CHANGELOG, ROADMAP, and a complete `.env.example`.

Everything the running game generates — art, narration, dialogue, growth,
journals, portraits — runs on local models (Qwen 2.5 7B AWQ via vLLM,
SDXL via ComfyUI) behind an in-process GPU arbiter. There is no cloud
API key anywhere in the runtime, tooling, or CI; heavier authoring is
pre-baked at design time and shipped as world data.
