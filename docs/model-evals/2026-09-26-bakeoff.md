# LLM bake-off, 2026-09-26

## Why

The runtime LLM was still Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct AWQ on vLLM 0.19.1, picked in
April 2026. Since then the Qwen3, Qwen3.5 and Gemma 4 families shipped, and vLLM
moved to 0.30. This run answers three questions:

1. Is there a local model that makes the game's words measurably better on this card?
2. Does it fit beside SDXL without giving up the VRAM headroom?
3. Is our inference approach still right: engine, flags, output format?

## Method

`bin/game model-eval run` (new, `daydream/model_eval.py`) drives every runtime LLM
surface through the production prompt builders and validators, against whatever vLLM
serves:

| suite | what passes |
|---|---|
| parser | Grounded parse over 48 phrasings (the tier_long probe corpus plus harder loft phrasings: two-object verbs, paraphrase, say vs talk, and fail-safe cases where the only correct outcome is no action on any in-scope object). The same post-processing as `parser._llm_parse`. |
| dialogue | 12 inputs x 3 loft NPCs through the production `talk` path. Fallback layers are attributed. Also checks brevity (at most 3 sentences / 400 chars), Bell's authored quest hint, point-of-view slips (narration outside the quote that says "you"), and a prompt-injection input scored on leakage only. |
| growth | 6 vision phrases through `validate_growth_output`. |
| journal | The journal probe's 5 scripted sessions through `journal.write_entry`. |
| retell | 10 wide-world lines x 2 tellings at production temperature through `retell.validate`. |
| examine | 6 unseeded objects through the lazy-cache describer. |
| drift | 3 NPCs x 3 moods. Must be one sentence, no quoted speech, at most 22 words. |
| json | The 5 strict-JSON drift prompts. |
| burst | One call alone vs three concurrent calls (the arbiter's LLM concurrency). |

Each challenger ran at production call settings (temperature 0 except retell's 0.8),
with thinking disabled server-side for Qwen3.x. All player-visible prose went into a
blinded sheet (`model-eval compare`: labels shuffled per item, key kept separately).
Two fresh subagents, which never saw which model was which, graded all 82 items
per model 1-5 against WHIMSY.md on two different shuffles. Inter-grader agreement on
the 230 shared non-dialogue outputs was r = 0.63.

The incumbent is deterministic run to run on this harness (identical scores across
three runs), so differences between models are signal, not sampling noise.

Qwen3.5's hybrid attention (Gated DeltaNet in 3 of every 4 layers) keeps KV cache in
only a quarter of its layers. At the unchanged 0.45 slice that still leaves 20.8k KV
tokens, 2.5 full 8192 contexts; real calls peak around 1.1k tokens. Qwen3 14B cannot
serve an 8192 context below about 0.60.

## Verdict

**Ship Qwen3.5 9B AWQ 4-bit (`cyankiwi/Qwen3.5-9B-AWQ-4bit`) on vLLM 0.30.0, at the
same 0.45 slice.**

- The words get measurably better: +0.69 blind prose, parser 41 to 47 of 48, zero
  point-of-view slips (the incumbent narrated five NPC replies as the player's body,
  e.g. "the ladder creaking under your weight"), growth validity 5/6 to 6/6.
- The VRAM envelope does not change.

**The cost is latency, and it is real.** A clicked `talk` goes from about 1.6 s to
about 2.7 s p50. A free-text parse that misses the deterministic fast-path goes from
0.6 s to 1.0 s. Both are inside the 1-5 s player-facing target, but the difference is
noticeable. This is the trade being proposed.

**Qwen3 14B is not chosen**, although its parser was perfect and its prose was not
distinguishable from the 9B. It needs a 0.60 slice. A portrait render left it about
0.65 GB of free VRAM on a card where the project has already been bitten by stochastic
OOMs. Revisit if SDXL moves out of residence (see BACKLOG `llm-14b-if-sdxl-offloaded`).

**Gemma 4 12B is not chosen**, although it wrote the best rooms and object
descriptions. It broke the two-sentence dialogue contract on a third of replies
(brevity 0.64), was the slowest (4.4 s dialogue p50, 1.9 s for a bare greeting), and
needs about 0.68 to serve 8192 tokens.

**Qwen3 8B is not chosen.** It fits the current stack with no engine change, but on
the production `talk` prompt 13/33 replies were bare, unquoted, unattributed speech
with no narration beat.

**Qwen3.5 4B is disqualified.** It grounded "take the moon" to `take` on the lantern:
a wrong action on a real object, the one failure the parser exists to prevent.

## Inference approach, re-checked

| question | measured | decision |
|---|---|---|
| vLLM 0.19.1 to 0.30.0, same model | identical scores and latency for Qwen 2.5 7B | Upgrade. Required for Qwen3.5 (Gated DeltaNet needs >= 0.20). |
| `--enforce-eager` vs CUDA graphs | 7B: no latency change, KV pool 58k to 43k. 9B hybrid: about 10% faster, graph pool 0.03 GiB | Graphs on. vLLM >= 0.21 profiles graph memory before sizing KV, which removes the old OOM reason. |
| `--kv-cache-dtype fp8_e4m3` on the 9B | no quality regression, no latency gain, fails to boot beside a resident SDXL | Stays off. Weights dominate bandwidth at ~1k-token prompts. |
| `response_format: json_schema` (enum verbs + in-scope ids) for the parser | Qwen3 8B 45 to 46 of 48. Qwen3.5 9B 47 to 47 | Not adopted. The remaining misses are semantic (wrong verb choice), which a schema cannot fix, and the post-processing already rejects invented ids. |
| llama.cpp / SGLang | not re-run here | localreview's 2026-08-19 bake-off on this card found llama.cpp competitive at matched model. It only unlocks dense 27B GGUFs, which decode at 8-11 tok/s. No reason to leave vLLM. |

Two stack findings:

- **Rebuild the venv on an engine bump.** An in-place pip upgrade from 0.19.1 to 0.30.0
  left 21 orphaned packages, including a whole CUDA 12 runtime beside the cu13 one and a
  `flashinfer-cubin` that FlashInfer's version check refuses. `pip check` passed anyway.
  `bin/vllm-bootstrap` now moves the stale venv aside and rebuilds.
- **FlashInfer needs `ninja` on PATH.** It JIT-compiles kernels at first use. The venv's
  `bin/` is now on the engine's PATH in `bin/game vllm-up`. This is the same silent-break
  risk localreview recorded.

## Model-fit adjustment: retell

At temperature 0 the 9B echoed 5 of 8 probe lines word-for-word. The validator
correctly rejects an unchanged line, so the authored text shows and nothing breaks, but
the retell layer went mostly quiet. Adding "always change it: returning the line
word-for-word is not a retelling" to the retell rules raised validity to 5/8 at
temperature 0 and 17/20 at the production 0.8. The rewordings stayed light and
fact-preserving ("The clasp is too cunning for your skill."). Sampling and prompt
defaults do not transfer between models, so this is part of adopting the model.

## Harness finding: the dialogue probes were measuring the wrong prompt

`data_skills.execute_by_name` resolves an NPC only through the legacy `t-<skill>`
convention. Envelope dialogue skills are named `dlg-tace` etc., so both the
benign-refusal probe and the first harness run exercised the second-person
dispatcher prompt instead of the third-person dialogue voice that `talk` uses. Every
model then narrated NPCs as "you". Both now call `data_skills.execute(..., npc=...)`
with talk's effect allowlist, like `verbs._handle_talk`.

## Known local limits carried forward

- **Growth transplants loft furniture.** Grown rooms sometimes carry the seed's motifs
  ("folded paper hour", the glass case, the ledger) into scenes where they do not
  belong. Both graders saw this across all models.
- **Retell is the weakest surface for every model.** Outputs are light rewordings, not a
  distinct wry voice. The authored-first rung limits exposure.
- **The 9B's dialogue runs a little long.** It averages about 108 output tokens against
  the incumbent's 83. Brevity is 0.94.

## What would change the answer

- A 9B-class model that holds this quality at the incumbent's latency.
- SDXL leaving permanent residence (offload between renders), which would free about
  6 GB. Qwen3 14B would then fit with margin.
- Ministral 3 8B/14B once vLLM's Pixtral path stops importing a symbol missing from its
  pinned transformers. It failed to load on 0.30.0 and was not evaluated.

Raw per-run results, every call, and both blind grading files are under
`~/data/daydream/model-eval/` (not in git).
